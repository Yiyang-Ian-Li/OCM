You are an agent in a household environment. Solve the task by selecting one valid text action at each step.

You will receive:
- The task description and the current observation.
- Environment feedback after each action.

The available actions are:
- go to {receptacle or location}
- take {object} from {receptacle}
- move {object} to {receptacle}
- open {receptacle}
- close {receptacle}
- toggle {object} {receptacle}
- clean {object} with {receptacle}
- heat {object} with {receptacle}
- cool {object} with {receptacle}
where {obj} and {recep} correspond to objects and receptacles.

Commands are location-sensitive. Go to the relevant receptacle before taking, moving, opening, closing, cleaning, heating, or cooling an object there. 
You should first think about the current condition and plan for your future actions.
